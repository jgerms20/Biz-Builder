The Framework

Six phases. No shortcuts.

Every business — regardless of category — goes through these six phases. The order matters. Skipping steps costs money. This is the sequence.

Phase 01

Concept

Define what this actually is. A one-liner, a mission, a target customer. Most businesses fail before they start because this never gets written down.

1–2 days
Phase 02

Formation

Make it official. Legal structure, EIN, business bank account. The boring stuff that protects everything you build after it.

1–2 weeks
Phase 03

Identity

Build the brand. Name, colors, typography, voice. People decide in seconds. Your identity determines what they decide.

3–7 days
Phase 04

Digital

Get online. Domain, business email, five core website pages, social handles. Your storefront is digital first, physical second.

1–3 weeks
Phase 05

Operations

Build how it runs. Services defined, pricing set, booking live, payment ready. A business without ops is a hobby with a logo.

3–5 days
Phase 06

Launch & Grow

Go live and build momentum. First clients, real testimonials, a referral mechanism. The only phase that never ends.

Ongoing
