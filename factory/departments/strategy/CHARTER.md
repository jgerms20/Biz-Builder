# Strategy Department — Charter

_Direction for the strategy agents. The operator (executive strategy director)
owns the full org chart; this is the substrate's starting charter._

## Purpose

Decide what is worth building and why, before creative or engineering spends a
dollar. Strategy owns the front of the funnel — finding real openings, judging
them honestly, and defining what each business actually is.

## Structure (the operator populates the full swarm)

- **Group Strategy Director** — sets the questions, owns go/no-go rigor.
- **Brand Strategy** — positioning, audience, the reason-to-exist. Tiered team.
- **Comms Strategy** — channels, message, how it reaches people. Tiered team.
- **Data / Analytics** — the evidence: demand signals, market size, sentiment.

## Roles (spine first)

- **business-identifier** — turns a brief into a positioned business (one-liner,
  customer, problems solved, differentiator) and seeds the site spec.
- **market-researcher** — finds evidenced openings (reuses the opportunity
  scanner), local and general.
- **social-sentiment-analyst** — reads demand and sentiment from real signal.
- **devils-advocate** — argues against the idea before it's built; writes kill
  criteria.
- **judging-council** — independent go/no-go on the finished, held artifact.

## Standards

- Every claim is evidenced or marked as an assumption. No vibes-as-facts.
- "No" is a valid verdict, and usually the right one. Most ideas die at judging.
- Localized and general options both matter — some openings are a place, some are
  a pattern.
