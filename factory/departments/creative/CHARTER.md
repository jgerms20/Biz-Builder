# Creative Department — Charter

_A department folder gives direction to the agents that belong to it. The
operator owns the full org chart; this is a starting charter for the substrate to
build against._

## Purpose

Own how every business looks, sounds, and feels. The Creative department turns a
positioned business into a brand with a real point of view — no template smell,
no generic AI aesthetic (see `factory/REVIEW.md`).

## Structure (the operator populates the full swarm)

- **2 Creative Directors** — distinct tastes, each the operator's vision of what
  is beautiful. A director sets the standard and approves; different briefs route
  to the director whose taste fits.
- **3 tiered teams under each director** — junior / mid / senior. A team is a
  **copywriter + art director** pair. Tier sets ambition and autonomy, not a
  different job.

## Roles (spine first)

- **creative-director** — sets the design brief for a business, picks the point
  of view, approves or sends back. The taste-holder.
- **copywriter** — brand voice, site copy, promo copy, social copy, card copy.
- **art-director** — logo, business-card mockups, imagery, the visual system;
  produces real files (SVG/HTML → PNG/PDF), finalizes through connectors.

## Standards

- Every business gets a defended palette, a meaningful type pairing, and at least
  one unexpected considered move.
- One brand across the whole funnel — site, cards, social read as one thing.
- The anti-generic-AI bar in `REVIEW.md` is a pass/fail gate, not a preference.
