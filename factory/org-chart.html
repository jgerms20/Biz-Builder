How to read it. Tiers (Jr / Mid / Snr) set ambition and autonomy, not a different job — a senior team takes the hardest briefs and needs the least direction. The bordered agents are the spine: one strong role per function, shipping now, before the full tiered teams are populated. This chart is yours to shape — here or in a separate agency repo.
